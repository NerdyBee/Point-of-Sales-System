1. Before you start

You need:

  • An Android phone or tablet. A tablet is more comfortable at the counter, but a phone works well too.
  • The Ajoke POS app file (an .apk file) from Ajoke Code Sphere, usually sent by WhatsApp or a download link.
  • An activation code for this device. You get it after you send your device code (section 3).
  • Optional: a Bluetooth thermal receipt printer (58 mm or 80 mm paper).

The app works without internet. You only need data or Wi-Fi to download the app, send receipts by WhatsApp and save backups online.

One licence = one device

Each licence only works on the phone or tablet it was activated on. If you change device, contact Ajoke Code Sphere for a new activation code. Back up your records first (section 19).

2. Install the app

  1. Open the .apk file you received (in WhatsApp, tap the file; or open it from Files → Downloads).
  2. Android may say the app is from an unknown source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway. The app is safe; it is just not from the Play Store.
  4. Tap Open. The Ajoke POS logo appears for a few seconds while the app starts.
Updating later

When you get a newer version, install it the same way, over the old one. Do not uninstall first, because uninstalling deletes the shop's records on the device.

3. Activate your licence

The first time the app opens, it shows the Activate Ajoke POS screen with a device code such as K7QF-29XM-C4TB.

  1. Tap Share device code and send it to Ajoke Code Sphere on WhatsApp (+234 803 415 0437) (or write it down carefully).
  2. You receive a long activation code back. Copy all of it; it is about 230 characters long.
  3. Paste it into the box Paste the activation code here and tap Activate.
  4. The app moves on to setting up your shop.
“This code is for device …, not this device”

The code was made for a different device code. Check that you sent the code shown on this device, then ask for a new one.

If your licence has an end date, the app warns you before it runs out. If it does expire, the app shows Renew your licence. Contact Ajoke Code Sphere to renew; you will get a new code to paste the same way, and your records stay as they are.

4. Set up your shop

Choose how this device will work:

OptionChoose it when
This device only (no server)Small shop, one phone or tablet. Everything is kept on the device. Most shops choose this.
Office server onlyThe shop has the Ajoke POS server on an office computer. Syncs over the shop Wi-Fi.
Cloud onlyThe business uses the online server. Syncs whenever there is internet.
Office server + cloudUses the office server on shop Wi-Fi and the cloud elsewhere.

“This device only” setup

  1. Enter the Business name (as you want it on receipts) and choose your Currency.
  2. Enter VAT % (use 0 if you do not charge VAT). Turn on Add a service charge only if you charge one.
  3. Enter the Owner's name, then a 6-digit PIN and Repeat PIN. Remember this PIN: it is the owner's key to everything.
  4. Tap Create business on this device.

Moving from another device? Tap Restore from a backup file instead and choose your backup file (section 19).

Linked to a server?

For the server options, a manager gets a Server address and Pairing code from the web app (Sync monitor → Pair a tablet). Enter them, tap Test connection, then continue. On a linked device, products, staff and reports are managed in the web app, and the bottom bar shows Sync instead of Backup.

5. Signing in and finding your way

Every time the app opens, or after someone taps Lock, it shows the sign-in screen. Tap your name and enter your PIN. Each staff member has their own PIN, so every sale shows who made it.

Lock when you step away

Tap the padlock Lock at the top so nobody can sell or see figures under your name.

Forgot your PIN?

  • Staff: ask an owner to set a new PIN in Manage → Staff.
  • Owner: tap your name, then Forgot PIN?. The app shows a request code. Tap Share request code and send it to Ajoke Code Sphere (+234 803 415 0437). After they confirm you are the owner, they send back a reset code. Paste it, tap Continue and choose a new PIN. Your records are not affected. The reset code works once, on this phone only.

Go to Manage → Products and tap + Product (the round button at the bottom right).

Correcting stock

Open the product and use the stock section: choose Received (+) or Remove (−), enter the Quantity and a reason (e.g. “Damaged”, “Counted”), then tap Save stock change. Every change is kept in the product's history. For new deliveries, use Inflow instead (section 14).

Products you no longer sell can be archived: they disappear from the Sell screen but stay in old sales and reports.

Add a second owner

8. Open the register

You must open the register before you can sell. This records how much cash was in the drawer at the start.

  1. Tap Register.
  2. Count the cash in the drawer and enter it as the Opening float (enter 0 if it is empty).
  3. Tap Open register. The app takes you to Sell.

The register stays open until you close it (section 18), even if you lock the app or switch the phone off.

9. Make a sale

  1. On Sell, tap products to add them. Use Search name, SKU or scan barcode to find items quickly, or tap a category.
  2. Each tap adds one. On a phone, tap the bar View sale at the bottom to see the cart. Change quantities with + and −, or remove a line.
  3. Optional: tap Add customer to attach a customer (needed for credit, and it earns them points). You can search by name or phone, or add a new one with name and phone.
  4. Tap Charge.
  5. Choose how the customer pays: Cash, Card, Transfer, Mobile money or On account (credit).
    • Cash: enter the Cash received; the app shows the Change to give.
    • Card, Transfer, Mobile money: enter the Payment reference (terminal slip or transfer reference). It is required, so you can match it later.
    • A customer can pay with more than one method, e.g. part cash, part transfer.
  6. Tap Complete sale. The receipt appears.
  7. Tap New sale for the next customer.
Stock is updated automatically

Every sale takes the items out of stock. If an item shows Out of stock, record a delivery (section 14) or correct the count (section 6).

10. Receipts: print or send by WhatsApp

After each sale, the receipt screen gives you:

  • Print: prints on your Bluetooth printer (set it up first, section 16).
  • Send (WhatsApp): makes a picture of the receipt and opens the share menu. Choose WhatsApp and the customer's chat. If the picture cannot be made, use Send as text.

Receipts show your logo, address and phone (section 17), the items, payments and, when a customer is attached, the customer's current account balance. You can print or send any receipt again later from Sales; it is marked REPRINT.

11. Selling on credit

Any registered customer can buy on credit (“on account”) and pay later, unless the owner has set them to cash only.

  1. Add the items and attach the customer with Add customer. You can also add the customer at the payment step with Choose customer.
  2. Tap Charge and choose On account. The app shows what the customer already owes and their limit.
  3. If they pay part now, enter it under Paid now (optional); the rest goes on their account.
  4. Tap Complete credit sale. The receipt shows the amount on account and the Account balance they now owe.

Credit limits

Owners and managers set this under Manage → Customers → customer → Details → Credit limit.

Credit limitMeaning
Blank (empty)No limit: the customer can owe any amount.
A number, e.g. 50,000The most they may owe. A sale that would go over it is refused, and nothing is saved.
0Cash only: no credit for this customer.
Credit is real money

Set limits for customers you do not know well, and check the Owing list regularly (section 12).

12. Customers, payments and statements

Go to Manage → Customers. The top shows the total owed to the shop and how many customers owe. Use the Owing filter to see only debtors. Tap a customer to open them; the Account tab shows Owes, Credit limit, Available and a dated Statement.

Receiving a payment

  1. On the customer's Account tab, tap Receive payment. The full amount owed is filled in; change it for a part payment.
  2. Choose Cash, Transfer, Card or Mobile money. For anything but cash, enter the Payment reference. Add a Note if useful.
  3. Tap Receive …. Cash payments need an open register; they are added to the cash expected in the drawer.
  4. The Payment recorded panel shows the receipt number (e.g. PAY-00001), previous balance, amount paid and balance owed. Tap Print or Send (WhatsApp), then Done.

To print or send an old payment receipt again, tap the payment in the Statement.

Tap Share statement to send the customer a full statement of credit sales, payments and balance by WhatsApp or SMS.

13. Sales history and voiding a sale

Tap Sales to see today's sales, newest first. Tap a sale to see its items and payments, and to Print receipt or Send (WhatsApp) again.

Voiding a sale (owner or manager)

If a sale was rung up wrongly, open it, tap Void sale, enter the Reason for voiding (e.g. “Wrong item rung up”) and confirm. The app:

  • puts the items back into stock,
  • takes any credit part off the customer's account,
  • keeps the sale on record marked Voided, with who voided it, when and why.

Voided sales are never deleted, so the history always adds up.

14. Recording new stock (Inflow)

When goods arrive from a supplier, record them so your quantities stay correct.

  1. Go to Manage → Inflow and tap Record inflow.
  2. Optional: enter the Supplier and the Invoice / waybill no.
  3. Use Find a product to add for each product received. Enter the Quantity received and, if you know it, the Cost per unit.
  4. Leave Update products' cost price on if the new cost should replace the old one.
  5. Tap Record inflow. Each delivery gets a number such as INF-00012 and the quantities go up at once.

Entered something twice? Open the inflow, tap Cancel inflow and give a reason. The quantities are taken back out and the inflow stays on record as Cancelled.

15. Stock report

Tap Reports. Choose a period (Today, Yesterday, This week, This month) or pick your own From and To dates.

ColumnMeaning
OpeningQuantity at the start of the period.
OutQuantity that left: sales and removals.
InQuantity that came in: inflows, returns and additions.
ClosingQuantity at the end of the period (Opening − Out + In).
  • Turn on Moved only to hide products that did not move in the period.
  • Use Filter products to find a product or category.
  • Tap Share to send the report as a spreadsheet file (CSV) that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
Check your stock regularly

Compare the Closing figure with what is really on the shelf. If they differ, correct the count on the product (section 6) with a reason such as “Counted”.

16. Setting up a receipt printer

Ajoke POS prints on most Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (58 mm or 80 mm).

  1. Switch the printer on and load paper.
  2. On the phone, open Android Settings → Bluetooth, find the printer and pair it. The PIN is often 0000 or 1234; see the printer's manual.
  3. In Ajoke POS, open Backup → Receipt printer, or tap the printer icon on any receipt screen.
  4. Under Connection choose Bluetooth (paired) and pick your printer. If it does not appear, try Bluetooth LE (scan).
  5. Choose the Paper width: 58 mm (small) or 80 mm (wide).
  6. Tap Print test page. If it looks right, you are ready.
SettingWhat it does
Print automatically after each salePrints the receipt as soon as a sale is completed.
Open cash drawer on cash salesFor drawers connected to the printer: opens the drawer on cash sales and cash payments.
Forget printerRemoves the printer so you can choose another.
Nothing prints?
  • Check the printer is on, has paper, and is not connected to another phone.
  • Allow the app's Nearby devices / Bluetooth permission when Android asks. If you refused it, go to Android Settings → Apps → Ajoke POS → Permissions.
  • Switch the printer off and on, then try Print test page again.
  • You can always Send (WhatsApp) the receipt instead.

17. Business details and logo

The owner can change these under Manage → Business:

  • Business name, Phone number and Address: shown at the top of every receipt.
  • Logo: tap Choose logo and pick a picture from the gallery. A simple logo on a white background prints best. Turn Print logo on receipts off if your printer is slow or the logo prints badly.
  • VAT %, Service charge % and the payment methods you accept.
  • Receipt footer: a line at the bottom of receipts, e.g. “No refund after payment. Thank you!”

Tap Save settings. A preview shows how the top of your receipt will look.

18. Close the register

At the end of the day (or end of each shift), count the cash and close the register.

  1. Tap Register. The screen shows the Opening float, Cash sales on this device, Customer payments (cash) and the Expected cash in drawer.
  2. Count the cash in the drawer and enter it under Counted cash.
  3. The app shows Balanced, Over or Short and by how much. Add a Note (optional) to explain any difference.
  4. Tap Close register.

Past shifts lists earlier shifts with who opened and closed them and any difference. Card, transfer and mobile money payments are not in the drawer, so they are not part of the expected cash.

19. Backup and moving to a new device

Your records live only on this device

If the phone is lost, stolen or broken and you have no backup, the records are gone. Make a backup at least once a week; the app reminds you after 7 days.

Make a backup

  1. Tap Backup → Back up now.
  2. The share menu opens with the backup file. Save it somewhere off the phone: Google Drive, email it to yourself, or send it to your own WhatsApp.

Restore on a new or repaired device

  1. Install Ajoke POS on the new device and activate it with a new activation code (section 3).
  2. On the setup screen choose This device only, then tap Restore from a backup file instead, and pick your latest backup file.
  3. Sign in with your usual PIN. Products, staff, customers, balances and sales are all back.

On a device already in use, Backup → Restore from backup file replaces everything on it with the backup. Erase this device deletes everything; make a backup first.

Good habits

  • Every staff member signs in with their own PIN, and taps Lock when leaving the counter.
  • Always enter the payment reference for transfers and POS card payments.
  • Record every delivery under Inflow on the day it arrives.
  • Close the register every day and look into any difference straight away.
  • Back up every week, and keep the backup off the phone.
  • Do not uninstall the app to update it; install the new version over the old one.
